Question 3 · Is retention improving?

About one in five customers buys again the next month

Whether it is improving, two years of data cannot say: early cohorts mix in old customers who came back, and the season moves month 1. A first draft claimed new customers fell 36%; with the same history window for both years it is 979 against 940, so the claim was withdrawn.
